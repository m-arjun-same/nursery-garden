# The Nursery Garden — website

A tropical garden oasis in South Los Angeles (3401 W. 63rd St, at Crenshaw) — home of the OnPointttt Caribbean food truck, The Garden Table five-course dinner series, plant preorders and private events.

Single-page site: garden story, 3D coverflow photo carousel, full truck menu with real prices, Garden Table courses with a working reservation builder (pick a menu + guest count, total updates, reserve by email or Toast), events, FAQ and visit details.

Photos are the garden's own, from its Instagram. Built with vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, GSAP ScrollTrigger + Lenis smooth scrolling.
